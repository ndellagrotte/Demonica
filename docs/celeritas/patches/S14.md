# S14: the terrain passes a shader pack expects

Draft, not sent. Replaces `seam.VintageRenderPassConfigurationBuilderMixin`
(group CORE_TERRAIN).

## Problem

forge122's `VintageRenderPassConfigurationBuilder.build` makes the passes vanilla
needs: solid, cutout (folded into the solid stage when passes are consolidated),
cutout-mipped and translucent. Shader packs expect more:
- **A separate water pass.** Packs draw water with `gbuffers_water` and other
  translucent terrain with its own program, and water must write depth so
  stacked water sorts.
- **Translucent terrain without depth writes.** Vanilla 1.12 draws the whole
  translucent stage with the depth mask off, so pass-1 block entities behind
  glass stay visible.
- **A way to tell passes apart.** `TerrainRenderPass` records no semantic, so a
  shader mod cannot tell water from other translucent terrain, or see whether a
  pass writes depth. Demonica tags its passes through `extraDefines`
  (`DEMONICA_PASS_SEMANTIC`, `DEMONICA_WRITES_DEPTH`; see `PassSemantics`), which
  also keeps the water pass distinct under `TerrainRenderPass.equals`.
- **A fluid material.** `RenderPassConfiguration` has no slot for the material
  fluids mesh into. Demonica keeps it in a side map
  (`ShaderPassConfigurations.fluidMaterial`), which the meshing patches (Phase 6)
  read.

## Proposal

- `VintageRenderSectionManager.create` uses `provider.passConfiguration(vertexType)`
  when it is non-null.
- Optionally: a `semantic` on `TerrainRenderPass`, and a fluid material on
  `RenderPassConfiguration`, so that no mod has to encode them in defines or side
  tables.

## What Demonica drops

The HEAD-cancel on `build`. `ShaderPassConfigurations` stays, as the provider's
implementation.
