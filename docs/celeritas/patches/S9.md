# S9: the block-entity phase

Draft, not sent. Replaces the `renderBlockEntities` injections in
`seam.CeleritasWorldRendererMixin` (group CORE_TERRAIN).

## Problem

forge122 replaces vanilla's block-entity loop in `RenderGlobal.renderEntities`
with `CeleritasWorldRenderer.renderBlockEntities(TileEntityRenderContext)`. The
points where a shader mod enters its block-entity phase in vanilla's loop are
gone, so block entities would be drawn with the programs of whatever phase was
current.

## Proposal

Call the provider's `beginBlockEntities()` and `endBlockEntities()` around the
body of `renderBlockEntities`. Low priority: the patch is a HEAD/RETURN pair on a
public method and only needs the method to keep its signature.

## Notes

The patch targets the full descriptor, not the erased bridge
`renderBlockEntities(Object)`, which calls it; injecting into both would enter
the phase twice.

The same two injections also time the block entities for Demonica's perf-debug
report (`block-entities-main`); that needs nothing from upstream.
